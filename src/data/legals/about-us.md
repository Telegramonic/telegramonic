# About Telegramonic

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

Welcome to **Telegramonic**, a modern cloud storage platform built directly on top of Telegram's robust, secure, and lightning-fast global infrastructure. Our goal is to transform the way you interact with cloud storage by providing a clean, drive-like interface powered entirely by Telegram.

---

## Why Telegramonic?

Traditional cloud storage providers limit your digital life with tight storage quotas, complex pricing tiers, and invasive data profiling. Telegramonic approaches cloud storage differently:

- **Limitless Space:** By utilizing Telegram's distributed data centers, we offer unlimited storage for all your files.
- **Privacy First:** We don't host your files on our own servers. Everything is saved securely inside Telegram's cloud.
- **Open and Simple:** No hidden fees, no artificial upload bottlenecks, and a design that feels right at home on any device.

---

## Core Features

| Feature | What It Means for You |
|---|---|
| **Infinite Storage** | Save files, media, and archives without worrying about running out of gigabytes. |
| **MTProto Encryption** | Your files benefit from the same robust security protocol that protects messages for 900M+ users. |
| **Lightning-Fast Speeds** | Dynamically download and upload files optimized for Telegram's distributed server network. |
| **Zero-Knowledge Architecture** | Telegramonic is a visual client; we never read, decrypt, or log your file contents. |

## Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize digital storage. We believe that everyone is entitled to private, secure, and unrestricted access to their files. By combining the power of Telegram's API with our clean file management UI, we give you full ownership of your data.

> **Privacy is a human right.** Telegramonic was built to help you claim that right with style, speed, and absolute reliability.
