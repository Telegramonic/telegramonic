## Technical Support Tips

To help us resolve your technical support issues as quickly as possible, please include the following details in your email:

1. Your **Telegram username** (if applicable)
2. The **device and OS version** you are using (e.g., iPhone 15 iOS 17, Mac Studio macOS Sonoma)
3. The **web browser** you are using (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
4. A **brief description** of the issue and steps to reproduce it
5. Any relevant **screenshots or error messages**

> **Note:** For your own safety, **never** share your Telegram login codes, passwords, or two-factor authentication password with anyone, including our support team. We will never ask for them.
