# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

Welcome to **Telegramonic**. Your privacy is not just a legal obligation for us — it is a core design principle. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our cloud storage platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create a Telegramonic account, we collect:

- Your **Telegram account identifier** (used solely for authentication)
- Display name and profile picture (synced from Telegram)
- Email address (if voluntarily provided)

### 1.2 Usage Data

We collect anonymized, aggregated usage metrics to improve performance:

- Feature interactions and navigation patterns
- File operation statistics (upload/download counts, not content)
- Device type and browser version

### 1.3 Files and Content

Your files are stored via **Telegram's MTProto infrastructure**. Telegramonic itself does **not** have access to the decrypted content of your files.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose                             | Legal Basis          |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Providing and improving the service | Legitimate interest  |
| Authentication and security         | Contract performance |
| Usage analytics (anonymized)        | Legitimate interest  |
| Legal compliance                    | Legal obligation     |

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share data only in the following circumstances:

1. **With Telegram** — as required to authenticate and store your files
2. **With service providers** — under strict confidentiality agreements for infrastructure only
3. **For legal compliance** — when required by law or valid court order

---

## 4. Data Security

Your security is built on multiple layers:

- All file transfers use **TLS 1.3** encryption in transit
- Files at rest are protected by **Telegram's MTProto** encryption
- Access tokens are short-lived and rotated regularly
- We maintain strict access controls and audit logs

> **You are the only person who can access your decrypted files.** We are architected to be zero-knowledge with respect to your content.

---

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectify** incorrect or outdated information
- **Erase** your account and associated data
- **Restrict** or object to certain processing
- **Portability** — export your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@telegramonic.com**.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Upon deletion:

- Account metadata is purged within **30 days**
- Anonymized analytics are retained for up to **24 months**
- Files stored via Telegram follow Telegram's own retention policies

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Telegramonic is not directed at individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email at least **14 days** before they take effect.
