# Introduction to Telegramonic

Telegramonic is a high-performance, minimalist cloud storage solution designed for digital craftsmen, developers, and tech professionals.

By leveraging Telegram's secure and distributed MTProto infrastructure, Telegramonic allows you to utilize unlimited storage capabilities directly through the API—without middlemen, third-party databases, or complex configurations.

## Core Features

- **Infinite Storage**: Break free from traditional cloud storage quotas.
- **Strict Security**: Encrypted peer-to-peer connection secured via MTProto 2.0.
- **Fast Uploads**: Parallel multi-part uploads with speeds up to 100MB/s depending on connection.
- **No Limits**: Upload individual files up to 4GB in size.

## Why MTProto?

Traditional cloud storage systems host your files on proprietary servers, exposing your data to potential breaches and privacy violations. Telegramonic establishes a direct secure tunnel between your client workspace and Telegram's distributed data centers, ensuring maximum security.
